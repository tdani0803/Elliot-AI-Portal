Jobs in your phone's calendar

Your ElliotAI jobs can show up in the calendar you already use. New jobs appear there automatically.

Add to iPhone calendar
Using Google Calendar?
  1. Tap Copy calendar link above.
  2. On a computer, open calendar.google.com.
  3. Next to Other calendars, click +, then From URL.
  4. Paste the link and click Add calendar. It shows on your phone too.

Google can take a few hours to show new jobs. Apple and Outlook are quicker.

Using Outlook?
  1. Tap Copy calendar link above.
  2. In Outlook, go to Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  3. Paste the link and save.

Keep this link private: anyone with it can see your jobs. To make changes, use ElliotAI — the calendar only shows them.
